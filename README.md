### Abstract
Autonomous driving has moved from the realm of science fiction to a very real possibility during the past twenty years,
due to rapid developments of radar technology and microprocessor capacity. Developers stand a better chance of repli-cating
the real-time decision-making of humans in autonomous cars. Autonomous cars have a variety of driver-assist and safety features,
such as automatic braking, lane departure warnings, and adaptive cruise control.
  This project investigates development and implementation of self-driving car systems, integrating various technologies
to achieve full self-driving capabilities. In Addition, this project aims to reduce traffic accidents caused by human negligence
and improve human resources. Our approach encompasses the design of a robust sensor suite, including Arduino Uno R3,
IR Sensors, HC-SR04 Ultrasonic Sensor, L298N Motor Driver and 4WD Smart Car Chassis, to enable comprehensive envi-ronmental perception.

### Hardware requirment ###
- Arduino UNO 3
- HC-SOR4 Ultrasonic Range Sensor
- IR Sensor (2 units)
- L298N Dual H-Bridge Motor Driver Module
- 4WD Smart Car Chassis Kit (Acrylic Frame)
- 18650 Li-ion Battery (2 units)
- Jumper Wires

Equitments shop `https://www.cestore-mm.com/`
